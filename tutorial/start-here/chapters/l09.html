⌘⇧M (or View → World Map) draws every entry on the shelf as a map: people, creatures, places and things, joined where your facts and links join them. Click a name to open its entry. Find narrows it to a name.

The map is flat by default. There’s a 3D view as well, which you can drag to turn. It’s mostly for delight.

Try this: open the World Map, find Ballast, and follow the line to Hesketh. Then switch to 3D and give the world a spin.
