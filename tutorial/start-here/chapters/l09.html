⌘K opens Go to Anything. Type a few letters and it finds chapters, passages, menu commands and, on a shelf that keeps a Canon, its entries and facts. Enter goes there; Esc puts the caret back where it was. It understands what you mean as well as what you typed: “bay mare” finds Juniper, and “snapshot” finds Take Snapshot.

⌘⇧M (or View → World Map) draws every entry on the shelf as a map: people, creatures, places and things, joined where your facts and links join them. Click a name to open its entry. Find narrows it to a name. The map is flat by default; there’s a 3D view as well, which you can drag to turn. It’s mostly for delight.

Try this: press ⌘K and type bay mare. Then open the World Map, find Ballast, and follow the line to Hesketh. Switch to 3D and give the world a spin.
