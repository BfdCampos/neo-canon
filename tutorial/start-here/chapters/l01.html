Welcome. This shelf is a small world to play in. Beside this notebook sits a short story, Across the Saltings: a courier, her mare and a letter that must cross a salt marsh before the tide. Nothing here is precious. Change anything you like; your own books are untouched.

NEO is a page and very little else. Type, and it saves as you go. The controls hide until you need them: move the pointer to the left edge of the window for the chapters (here, the lessons), to the right edge for notes, and to the bottom for the tabs. ← Back to Shelf, at the top left, takes you back to your books, and so does Esc.

Press Enter twice at the end of a paragraph for a section break, and three times for a new chapter. A sentence you love but have to cut goes to Darlings rather than the bin: select it and press ⌘⇧D, or drag it onto the Darlings tab. The Outline tab shows the book as index cards, one per section. ⌘S takes a snapshot of the whole book, and the Darlings tab keeps the list.

Try this: click ← Back to Shelf and open Across the Saltings. Don’t start reading yet: click the Darlings tab at the bottom instead. One sentence was cut from the story already, and it’s waiting there. Then come back to this notebook for lesson 2.
