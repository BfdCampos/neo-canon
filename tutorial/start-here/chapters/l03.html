Every book has a Canon tab at the bottom of the window, between Outline and Darlings. On this shelf it holds six entries already: Wren Calloway, Juniper, Hesketh, Ballast, Marrowby and the Saltings.

Entries are listed on the left, by kind. Click one and the page shows its facts. The dot beside a fact says how far to trust it: filled when Canon checked it against its source, hollow when it hasn’t yet, a pen nib when you wrote it yourself.

Under each fact is its source, the exact words from the story. Click a source and NEO opens that chapter with the words lit.

Entries are plain Markdown files in your library folder, one per entry. You can read them, or edit them, in any text editor.

Try this: open Across the Saltings, click the Canon tab, choose Juniper, and click the source under “Sex: female (mare)”.
