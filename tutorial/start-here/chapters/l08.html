⌘K opens Go to Anything. Type a few letters and it finds chapters, passages, menu commands and, on a shelf that keeps a Canon, its entries and facts. Enter goes there; Esc puts the caret back where it was.

It understands what you mean as well as what you typed: “bay mare” finds Juniper, and “snapshot” finds Take Snapshot.

Try this: press ⌘K and type bay mare. Then press ⌘K again and type world map.
