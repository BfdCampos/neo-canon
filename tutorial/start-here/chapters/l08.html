Canon is a little like the tools programmers use to find where a thing is defined and where it’s used. Here the things are people and places.

In an entry, the part called In my words is yours alone; Canon never writes there. Type a name in double square brackets, like [[Juniper]], and it becomes a link. The entry it points to lists the link under Linked from. Below that, Mentioned in lists every passage that names the entry, in reading order.

A link to a name with no entry yet is underlined with dots. Click it to make the entry.

While you write, the right-hand pane shows In this chapter: who and what the chapter names, with the people in the paragraph you’re writing picked out.

Try this: open Wren Calloway’s entry. Under In my words there is a line already; click at the very end of it, press Enter, and type: Carries a letter for [[Odile Fenn]]. Click anywhere outside the box: the brackets become a link. Click the new link and make an entry for her. (If Odile already has one, the link simply opens it.)
