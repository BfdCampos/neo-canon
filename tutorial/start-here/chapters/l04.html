Before this lesson, read Chapter 2 of Across the Saltings. It’s short. Then come back.

Every book has a Canon tab at the bottom of the window, just before Darlings. On this shelf it holds six entries already: Wren Calloway, Juniper, Hesketh, Ballast, Marrowby and the Saltings. No reader needed: the tutorial wrote them down for you. (Canon knows the whole story, so an entry may quote Chapter 3 as well. If you’d rather not peek, finish the story first.)

The entries are listed in the pane on the left, by kind, and the pane on the right holds Needs You and a small map of the world. On the Canon tab both stay open; the small circle at the top of a pane unpins it. Click an entry and the page shows its facts. The dot beside a fact says how far to trust it: filled when Canon checked it against its source, hollow when it hasn’t yet, a pen nib when you wrote it yourself.

Under each fact is its source, the exact words from the story. Click a source and NEO opens that chapter with the words lit.

Below the facts, Story so far lists what has happened to them, a line for each key point with its passage, in reading order. The reader writes these as it reads; a few came with the tutorial.

Entries are plain Markdown files in your library folder, one per entry. You can read them, or edit them, in any text editor.

Try this: open Across the Saltings, click the Canon tab, choose Juniper on the left, and click the source under “Sex: female (mare)”. Then go back to the Canon tab, open Hesketh and read his Story so far.
