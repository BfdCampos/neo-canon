Everything so far works with facts written down, by you or by this tutorial. To find facts for itself, Canon needs its reader: a small language model that runs on this computer. It’s one download, a few gigabytes, and afterwards it works offline. Nothing about your books ever leaves the machine.

With the reader, Read the Shelf reads every book and notebook and proposes entries and facts. Each one must quote its source word for word, or Canon throws it away. A fact is only marked checked when the text shows it. Anything less sure waits under Needs You in the Canon tab’s right-hand pane, for you to answer That’s right, Not right, or Skip. Words a character says are kept as what they say, never as the truth.

The reader pauses the instant you type, and carries on after ten seconds of quiet.

Try this: on the Canon tab, answer the one question waiting under Needs You: is Juniper Wren’s horse? If you’d like the reader, Help → Welcome to NEO Canon… shows the one to download.
