With the reader, Read the Shelf on the Canon tab reads every book and notebook and proposes entries and facts. Each one must quote its source word for word, and the words must tie it to its name, or Canon leaves it out. The first thing your books or notes say about someone is kept without asking: you wrote it. A later passage that says otherwise is a slip, not a question.

So Needs You, in the Canon tab’s right-hand pane, holds only two kinds of question: your notes and your story disagree (Which is right?, with both passages), or a character only says something. Words a character says are kept as what they say, never as the truth.

The reader also keeps the story’s key points under Story so far on each entry: who did what, one short line, with the passage. They are Canon’s notes, never words for your book, and you can change any of them. If a later chapter gives the deed to someone else (“It was Dave who touched the vortex”), the Continuity Pass marks the name.

The reader pauses the instant you type, and carries on after ten seconds of quiet.
