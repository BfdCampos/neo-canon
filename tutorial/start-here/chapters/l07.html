Sometimes the slip is the fact. You decide Juniper should have been a stallion all along. Canon can find every line that says otherwise, across every book on the shelf, and let you change them one at a time.

On the Canon tab, open Juniper and hover the fact Sex: female (mare). Choose Edit, change it to Sex: stallion and press Enter. Canon asks Change It Across the Series?: Find Passages shows a review list in the right-hand pane, each passage with the change marked; Just Change the Fact leaves the books alone. In the list, Apply a change, Skip it, or Edit… to put it in your own words. Lines that need more than a word swapped say so, and wait for you. (A slip about Juniper, when there is one, offers the same from its popover: Juniper is now male…)

Before the first change in each book, NEO takes a snapshot. ⌘Z undoes a change; Darlings → Snapshots brings back the whole book as it was.

Try this: make Juniper a stallion. Apply one change, Skip one, then press ⌘Z and watch the change you applied come back to the list.
