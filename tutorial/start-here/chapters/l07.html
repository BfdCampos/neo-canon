Sometimes the slip is the fact. You decide Juniper should have been a stallion all along. Canon can find every line that says otherwise, across every book on the shelf, and let you change them one at a time.

Hover a slip about Juniper and choose Juniper is now male…, or change the fact on Juniper’s entry. Canon asks which word you mean (stallion, gelding, colt, or just male), then shows a review list: each passage, with the change marked. Apply it, Skip it, or Edit… to put it in your own words. Lines that need more than a word swapped say so, and wait for you.

Before the first change in each book, NEO takes a snapshot. ⌘Z undoes a change; Darlings → Snapshots brings back the whole book as it was.

Try this: make Juniper a stallion. Apply one change, Skip one, then press ⌘Z and watch the change come back to the list.
