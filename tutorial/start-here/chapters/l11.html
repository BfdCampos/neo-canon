That’s all of it. Now for your own world.

Put the books of one world on a shelf of their own, then open any of them and choose the Canon tab: Keep a Canon is one button. (Or right-click the shelf’s name.) Keep a series together; give a standalone novel a shelf of its own, so two worlds never mix.

For world-building, right-click the shelf’s + and choose New Notebook. A notebook is pages with names, like this one, and Canon reads it alongside the books. A page that’s only a maybe can be left out: right-click its name and choose Leave Out of Canon. (These lessons are left out, so they never count as your world.)

Help → Open the Tutorial brings you back here whenever you like. Help → Start the Tutorial Afresh puts a clean copy on the shelf and sets this one aside, with your changes kept.
