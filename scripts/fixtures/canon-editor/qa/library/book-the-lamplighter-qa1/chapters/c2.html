Morning came grey and cold over Dunmere. Mara woke before Tobias and made tea in the lamp-room, and her brown eyes were red from too little sleep.

Tobias came up the stairs yawning. He rubbed her eyes with the heel of her hand and held out the taper he had carried all night.

The Lantern House had nine floors, and Mara said they were nine too many for one woman and a boy. She said it every morning. Tobias had stopped listening in the spring.

It was Tobias who lit the great lamp that night, and Mara let him, standing back with the brass key in her fist.
