Visitors still asked to see the bell that Young Joss had cast in 1889.

The old man’s blue eyes were the first thing strangers noticed about him.

That autumn Constable Pryce came to the yard about the smoke.

He came past every morning at nine now, since the station had moved to Castle Road.

Young Joss had blue eyes, like his mother.

The boy’s red hair was full of sand from Bay 9.

Bellows slept behind the furnace, as he always had.

Pryce was forty that autumn too.
