The Petrel was cramped and loud, and it smelt of Voss’s coffee. Through the forward port the vent field glowed a dull orange in the black water.

Nine rubbed her green eyes and tried to read the depth gauge. Voss whistled through his teeth, steering with one hand; his black beard was flecked with grey in the instrument light.

“The Old Woman likes you,” Voss said. “Don’t let it go to your head.”

Commander Okafur’s voice came over the radio at the second hour, calm and dry, asking for a reading.

They came back through Dock Three with eleven minutes to spare. Ada was waiting at the top of the Spine with two mugs, and her green eyes were red from not sleeping.

That night Nine lay in bunk nine and listened to the pumps, and for once she did not mind the hum.
