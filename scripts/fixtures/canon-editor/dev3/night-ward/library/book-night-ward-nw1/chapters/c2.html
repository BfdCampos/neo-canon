Raff came on at eight, as she did every night, and hung her coat in the sluice.

Larch Ward was on the third floor of the west wing. The lift had been broken since Easter.

She took Mrs Abbott’s tea along to Room 41 at midnight, and found her asleep with the lamp on.

Gerald Abbott, who sold insurance in Swindon, came at nine with chrysanthemums.

Okoro: told you. Barrister

Raff: insurance, you muppet
