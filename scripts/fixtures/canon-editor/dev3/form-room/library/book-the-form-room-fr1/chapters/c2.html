Assembly in the Great Hall was at half past eight, as it was every morning, and Hester was late for it.

The science block, next to the sports hall, had been locked since the fire.

Nobody had touched the cedar that Hester Pell had planted on the front lawn.

Miss Pell taught biology to Year Eleven, as she had for six years.

The girls were frightened of Mr Wilde, who never raised his voice.
