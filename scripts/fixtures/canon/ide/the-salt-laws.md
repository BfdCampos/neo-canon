# The Salt Laws

## Facts
- Who may sell: the council of Corwick, under its seal · yours
  - Written in Canon
- Penalty: a hand, for the second offence · yours
  - Written in Canon

## In my words
Salt is sold only under the seal of [[Corwick]]'s council, and the convoys carry it.
What comes up the coast road from [[Saltmere|the coast]] pays the wall tax twice.
