Clover would not go past the dunes on the way back from the market. Mara coaxed her for an hour, and then gave up. She slid the halter off the bull and let the beast graze at the roadside while she sat on the wall and ate her bread.

It was dark before they came over the headland. The light was turning above the rock, and in the cliff field Hector bellowed at it as it came round.

Jory was waiting at the gate with a lantern. He had kept the light for nine nights and had not once looked down.

The next week the board’s letter came. It said that the keeper had acted properly in every respect, and that a new pin for the clockwork would be sent by the spring carrier. She read it twice and put it in the logbook, between the pages for the night of the wreck.
